2024power systemsIEEE publication

Load Flow with Smart-Inverter Voltage Control

Traditional power-flow analysis ignores what modern smart inverters actually do. I extended the classic algorithm on an IEEE 14-bus network to model Volt-VAR and Volt-WATT control modes — adjusting the Jacobian and power injections so the analysis reflects how renewable-driven grids behave under disturbance.
